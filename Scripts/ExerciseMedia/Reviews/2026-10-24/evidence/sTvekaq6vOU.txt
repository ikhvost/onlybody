  - text: The high knees exercise is a fast paced cardio exercise which gets your heart rate up and burns calories, so it's great for HIIT workouts, warm ups, or keeping your heart rate high during training sessions. It can also help to increase strength, power, and endurance in your lower body, and improve your balance and stability. 1. Stand with your feet around hip-width apart. 2. Hold your forearms out in front of you, palms facing the floor, with your elbows at a 90 degree angle. 3. While standing on the spot, quickly bring your right knee up to meet your palms. 4. Drop your leg back down and quickly bring your left knee up. 5. Alternate between both legs for your desired reps or total time. Find more exercise demonstrations here 👉
