  - text: "Learn how to do the perfect side plank from the most trusted name in fitness, the National Academy of Sports Medicine. Steps for Doing a Side Plank:"
  - list:
    - listitem: Lie on your side with the feet and legs on top of each other, the forearm on the ground, and the elbow under the shoulder.
    - listitem: Draw in the belly button and squeeze the butt muscles.
    - listitem: Lift the hips and legs off of the ground until the body forms a straight line from head to toe, resting on the forearm and feet.
    - listitem: Lift the top arm up into the air with your palm facing away from you.
    - listitem: Bring your arm down.
    - listitem: Lower the rest of your body to the starting position.
    - listitem: Repeat for the desired number of repetitions before switching sides.
