Here's how to it properly:
1. Get yourself set up by attaching a rope to the low pulley of your trusty cable machine.
2. Face that machine head-on, grab hold of that rope with both hands, and then pivot so you're facing away from the machine. Keep those elbows bent, and the rope should be positioned behind you and overhead.
3. Now, lock those elbows into place—you want to keep them nice and steady throughout.
4. Time to activate those triceps! Straighten those arms up vertically overhead, really squeezing those triceps and pulling that rope apart as you extend.
5. Once you've hit the top, slowly bring those arms back down to the starting position with control.

And there you have it—repeat for that killer triceps burn. Let's crush it!
