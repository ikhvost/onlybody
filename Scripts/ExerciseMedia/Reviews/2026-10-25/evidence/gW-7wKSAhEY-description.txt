Here’s how to do it with perfect form:
1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart, holding a pair of dumbbells in front of your thighs with an overhand grip.
2. Keep your knees slightly bent and your core braced. 
3. Start the movement by hinging at your hips, pushing your butt back, and lowering your torso until it’s almost parallel to the floor. 
4. Keep your back flat and your neck in a neutral position.
5. Don’t go any lower if your lower back starts to round—protect that spine!
6. Keep the dumbbells close to your legs as you lower them, then engage your hamstrings and glutes to drive your hips forward and return to the starting position.
7. Repeat for your desired number of reps.

Focus on control, feel the stretch, and crush those posterior chain gains!
