													224 text The close grip barbell bench press has a slightly different movement pattern to the traditional bench press, and as a result activates the triceps and anterior deltoids to a greater degree. Watch our Personal Trainer for how to do a close grip barbell bench press.

1. Lie down on the bench so that the barbell is above your face and your feet are planted firmly on the floor
2. Grip the barbell with your fingers and thumb, hands shoulder-width apart
3. Press the barbell up slightly to take it off the rack, and then push it towards the ceiling by extending your arms straight up above your shoulders
4. Slowly lower the barbell, keeping your elbows close to your body and stopping when it is just above your chest
5. At the lower half of the exercise, the barbell should be in line with your sternum 
6. Repeat for the reps you have planned
