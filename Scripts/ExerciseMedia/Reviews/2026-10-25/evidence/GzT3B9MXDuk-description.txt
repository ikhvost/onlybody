													224 text The Wide Grip Weighted Pull Up is an excellent exercise to target the back. Using a wider grip makes it more challenging and can help build a wider back. You can add weight to your pull up in many different ways. In this exercise demo I’m adding weight to a dip belt.
To get started:
1. Once you have added the weight, grip the handles of pull up bar with a wide grip and hang at arm’s length.
2. Contract your lats and think about pulling your elbows down to pull your chest to the bar. 
3. Pause, then under control, lower your body and fully extend your arms back to the starting position. 
4. Repeat for reps.
