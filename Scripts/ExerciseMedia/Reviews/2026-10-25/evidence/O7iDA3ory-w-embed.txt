													225 text Hanging knee raises, or hanging tucks, work the hip flexors and core and can improve grip strength. 

Watch Personal Trainer Tom demonstrate how to do hanging knee raises here.

1. Using a pronated (overhand) grip, grab onto a pull up bar with your hands shoulder width apart. Allow your shoulder blades to drop and tuck your pelvis towards your bottom ribs.  
2. Bend your knees so they are at a 90 degree angle and your thighs are parallel with the floor and brace your core. 
3. Breathe out as you raise both knees up towards your chest, stopping when you can no longer squeeze them any closer. 
4. Breathe in as you slowly lower both legs back down to the starting position.
