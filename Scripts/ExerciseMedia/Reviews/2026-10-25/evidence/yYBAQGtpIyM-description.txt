Here's how to do it properly:
1. Plant the balls of your feet firmly on the platform, aligning your shoulders under the shoulder pads for stability.
2. Depending on your target, adjust your toes to the following positions: Keep them straight to hit that overall gastrocnemius, turn them in to focus on the outer edge, rotate them out to hone in on the inner portion.
3. With a slight bend in your knees, rise up on your toes as high as possible, really squeezing those calves at the peak.
4. Hold that contraction for a beat, then lower back down slowly, feeling that satisfying stretch through the calves.
5. Keep those knees locked in place throughout—no wobbling allowed.
6. Rinse and repeat for maximum calf-pumping action.
