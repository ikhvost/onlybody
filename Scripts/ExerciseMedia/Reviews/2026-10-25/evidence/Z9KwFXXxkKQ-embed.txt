Here's how to do it properly:
1. Position yourself in the center of a cable machine setup with handles attached to low pulleys.
2. Grab each handle with an overhand grip using the opposite hand, so your arms form a crossed position in front of you.
3. Engage your core, keep your chest up, and maintain a stable stance.
4. Lift both handles with your arms extended until they're slightly above parallel to the ground.
5. Focus on maintaining a position where your pinkie fingers are higher than your thumbs, as if you're pouring water out of a jar.
6. Under control, lower the handles back down without releasing tension in your shoulders.
7. Repeat this movement for a great shoulder workout!
