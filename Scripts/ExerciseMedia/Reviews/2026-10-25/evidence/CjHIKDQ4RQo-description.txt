													237 text The bench press is a great upper body compound exercise which works the chest, triceps, and shoulders when done properly. Getting the correct form ensures these muscles are targeted and helps to prevent injuries. Learn how to do a barbell bench press by watching our Personal Trainer here. 

Most bench press racks use a 20kg Olympic barbell so you will need to be comfortable pressing this weight before using this equipment. Alternatively, you can perform the following steps using a lighter barbell on a flat bench. 

1. Lie down on the bench so that the barbell is above your face and your feet are planted firmly on the floor
2. Grip the barbell with your fingers and thumb, hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart
3. Press the barbell up slightly to take it off the rack, and then push it towards the ceiling by extending your arms upwards
4. Slowly lower the barbell, stopping when it is just above your chest
5. At the lower half of the exercise, the barbell should be in line with the bottom of your chest
6. Repeat for the reps you have planned
