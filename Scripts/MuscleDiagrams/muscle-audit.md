# Muscle classification audit — 2026-10-24

Primary means a main mover or the principal isometric target; secondary means a meaningful assistant/stabilizer. Multiple primaries do not imply equal force or equal hypertrophy. These broad tags are editorial classifications based on joint actions, not per-exercise EMG measurements for all 65 variants.

## Variant assumptions and references

- Dips means parallel bars, not bench dips: chest and triceps are main movers; shoulders assist. [Bar-dip study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9659300/) and [bench/bar/ring comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36293792/).
- Push-up variations retain chest and triceps. Narrow hands do not remove the chest. [Hand-position study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16095413/) and [NASM push-up guide](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/push-up). Pike/handstand variants shift the principal press toward shoulders and triceps.
- Pull-ups/rows combine shoulder pulling and elbow flexion, so Back and Biceps are primary; Forearms covers grip. Scapular pull-ups keep elbows straight, so Biceps is not a primary target.
- Scapular push-ups emphasize Serratus through scapular protraction. [Push-up-plus systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6863690/). Core denotes bracing, not the serratus.
- Squat/lunge patterns combine knee and hip extension: Quads and Glutes. Cossack/lateral variations additionally load Adductors. [NASM Bulgarian split squat](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/bulgarian-split-squat) lists quads/glutes and supporting core, hamstrings and adductors.
- Leg-raise variants combine hip flexion with active pelvic control. Hip Flexors and Core are primary for hanging raises; Flutter Kicks emphasizes hip flexion with Core supporting. [Leg-lift study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9118976/). These tags assume controlled pelvic positioning; simply swinging the legs changes the exercise.
- Hip hinges use Hamstrings and Glutes; bridges emphasize Glutes; knee-flexion curls emphasize Hamstrings. Calves includes gastrocnemius and soleus: bent knees change their relative contribution without inventing a separate region. [Standing/seated calf study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38156065/).
- Burpees assumes a push-up plus jump. Jumping/walking jacks, crawls and high knees list their specific locomotor/stabilizing targets while retaining Full Body as a category.
- Support muscles are selected meaningful roles, not an exhaustive list of every active muscle. Forearms describes gripping; Biceps/Triceps describe upper-arm roles. Back remains a broad surface grouping (including spinal extensors for Superman/Bird Dog).
